# Code of conduct

Precedent follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and make room for
newcomers. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else this project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at burhanmujahid552@gmail.com. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or ban participants who break
these rules.
