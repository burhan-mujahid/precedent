Five checks outside the model

The first to fail hands it to a person.

  1. Coverage

    No approved rule covers it.

  2. Judgement

    The model says the rules don’t settle it.

  3. Grounding

    A cited rule it wasn’t shown, or an invented number.

  4. Confidence

    Below your bar for that risk level.

  5. Risk

    Money, legal, health or data: a person signs off.

Questions people ask

How is this different from Parlant or Mem0?

They run rules developers write, or remember users. Precedent learns and tests yours.

What if the agent goes further than what was approved?

It re-checks before each step and after Done. A new amount, a riskier action, a changed rule or a stale approval halts the run for good.

Where does our data go?

Secrets and personal data are stripped first. Offline mode keeps everything local.

Does it fit our automations?

Python, MCP, or precedent ask --json from n8n or Make.
